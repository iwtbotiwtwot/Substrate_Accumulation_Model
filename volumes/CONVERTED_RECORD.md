# The pdftotext conversions, retired (2026-10-25)

From 19 to 25 October 2026 the living texts of Volumes I–III were pdftotext conversions of the 19 October PDFs with prose appended under them. On 25 October the
living texts became the typeset sources (`volumes/<V>/src`), and the conversions moved to `volumes/<V>/converted/` as the record of what was written in that
week: the received-results sections (whose content is now in the chapters as source text), the Volume I drafted ceiling section (now Chapter VIII Section 18), the
Volume II binding-readout ledger (now Chapter V Sections 13–15), and the Volume III Part 2 declaration (now Part 2 of the built volume). The Volume I trim of
25 October (Option A) was applied to the conversion first and to the source the same day; the source build is the edition. Nothing is deleted; git history holds
every state. Directed by Sean Brady. Executed with Claude Code.
