\subsection{Three mathematical forms of a returned value}
\label{v3-runtime-value-types}

An exact rational, an exact finite logarithmic expression and a certified interval answer different questions. Rational arithmetic can return $7/3-4/3=1$ without approximation. A logarithmic calculation can retain $\log2$ as an exact expression even though a finite decimal representation is unavailable. A numerical consumer can then enclose that expression between exact rational endpoints at a declared working precision. The interval is the returned numerical object; its width records the remaining certified uncertainty.

The native scalar contract accepts integers and exact rational or decimal strings. Interval endpoints are exact lower and upper values. Binary floating-point operands are not silently promoted into scientific exactness. Administrative host timing has a separately typed finite representation and does not supply mathematical source data. This separation lets the same engine manage deadlines while preserving the exact scalar semantics of its calculations.\samref{V3R02}

A GEN5 logarithmic expression is a rational-weighted sum
\[
 \mathcal L=c+\sum_jq_j\log r_j,
 \qquad r_j>0,\quad q_j\in\mathbb Q.
\]
Arguments can remain factored powers. An exponent such as $10^{18}$ need not be expanded into the corresponding enormous integer before its logarithm is represented. Canonical factor cancellation gives
\[
 \log12-\log3-2\log2=0
\]
as an exact normalized result. Dimensioned inputs provide a compatible positive reference, so logarithms act on dimensionless ratios with their source meaning attached.\samref{V3R02}

Signed combinations preserve cancellation before numerical exponentiation. Equal positive and negative contributions can disappear exactly; small remaining differences use stable source operations such as \texttt{expm1}. Increasing precision then improves the arithmetic enclosure of the surviving expression. It does not remove a declared source radius, alter a physical unit or infer a missing reference.

This distinction appears directly in the completed precision controls. The normalized expression $\log4-2\log2$ cancels exactly at 64 bits. The positive quantity $\log(1+2^{-400})$ is also certified at 64 bits through the stable logarithm-one-plus path. The apparent number of decimal zeros in an input is consequently an incomplete guide to the arithmetic work. Algebraic structure, cancellation and the requested observable determine the useful route.\samref{V3R04}

\subsection{A source scalar and a spectral profile are different logarithms}
\label{v3-runtime-source-log}

The certified GEN3 source-log construction makes the target explicit. Its registered mean/cut source admits an ordered rational vector $a_0,\ldots,a_{s-1}$. Put $M=\sum_i a_i$. The source quantity is
\[
 Q=\frac{M^2}{s}
 +\sum_{r=1}^{s-1}
 \frac{\left(\sum_{i<r}a_i-rM/s\right)^2}{r(s-r)}.
\]
The native record's \texttt{mean} field retains the signed sum $M$. Its name is not an instruction to replace $M$ by $M/s$.\samref{V3R05}

The same registered kernel has exact eigenvalue rule
\[
 \lambda_0=1,\qquad
 \lambda_k=\frac1{k(k+1)}\quad(k\geq1).
\]
Projection onto its polynomial modes gives weights
$w_k=A_k^2/\|p_k\|^2$. The scalar target and spectral target are
\[
 \log(1+Q),\qquad
 \sum_kw_k\log(1+\tau\lambda_k).
\]
They are separately typed readouts, and neither is a log determinant. For the source $(1,-1)$ and $\tau=1$, the complete spectral expression is $2\log(3/2)$, while the scalar expression is $\log2$. The difference is mathematical content, not numerical disagreement.\samref{V3R05}

After modes through $m$ have been retained, the exact residual mass is
\[
 R_m=\sum_i a_i^2-\sum_{k=0}^m w_k.
\]
It bounds remaining source energy by $R_m\lambda_{m+1}$. Full degree has zero tail; a negative residual is rejected. Adaptive refinement adds missing modes instead of recomputing the retained projections. New values of $\tau$ reuse the same source modes. A direct source-cut route separately evaluates complete $Q$ without requesting spectral projections.

This eigenvalue rule belongs to the admitted mean/cut kernel. It is not imposed on an arbitrary exchange matrix because both happen to possess spectra. The source contract retains basis, dimensionless units, reference and ordered history. The logarithm consequently acts on a specified source quantity, while other operators use their own registered spectral definitions.\samref{V3R05}

Numerical log evaluation uses rational range reduction and an explicit-tail rational atanh series. A tolerance-met return, a valid budget-limited enclosure and an exact symbolic expression remain distinct result states. Exhausting a term or mode budget retains the last valid certificate. An overlapping interval does not become an exact equality or ordering. These behaviors give a later consumer enough information to choose whether it needs more arithmetic, more source modes or merely the existing expression.\samref{V3R05}
