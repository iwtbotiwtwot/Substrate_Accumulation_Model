\subsubsection{Rational sphere chart}\label{viii-ch10-rational-sphere-chart}

Set \(x=\tan(\chi/2)\). The point

\[
S(x)=\left(X(x),Z(x)\right)
=\left(\frac{2x}{1+x^2},\frac{1-x^2}{1+x^2}\right)
\]

because

\[
X(x)^2+Z(x)^2
=\frac{4x^2+(1-x^2)^2}{(1+x^2)^2}
=\frac{1+2x^2+x^4}{(1+x^2)^2}
=1.
\]

The two limits \(x=0\) and \(x=\infty\) close the chart at reciprocal
poles.

\subsubsection{Multiplicative actions and
reversal}\label{viii-ch10-multiplicative-actions-and-reversal}

Define

\[
T_n(x)=nx.
\]

Then operator composition is exactly integer multiplication:

\[
(T_m\circ T_n)(x)=m(nx)=(mn)x=T_{mn}(x).
\]

With \(J(x)=x^{-1}\),

\[
(J\circ T_n\circ J)(x)
=J\!\left(\frac n x\right)
=\frac x n
=T_{1/n}(x).
\]

Thus reversal conjugates expansion by \(n\) to contraction by \(1/n\).

Differentiating \(x=\tan(\chi/2)\) gives

\[
\frac{dx}{x}
=\frac{\tfrac12\sec^2(\chi/2)}{\tan(\chi/2)}d\chi
=\frac{d\chi}{2\sin(\chi/2)\cos(\chi/2)}
=\frac{d\chi}{\sin\chi}.
\]

Therefore

\[
du=\frac{dx}{x}=\frac{d\chi}{\sin\chi}.
\]

The logarithmic coordinate \(u=\log x\) converts \(T_n\) into
translation by \(\log n\), joining the sphere chart to the logarithmic
translation language of Reciprocal History without merging their
application authorities.

\subsubsection{Deriving the candidate
form}\label{viii-ch10-deriving-the-candidate-form}

Let \(p\) be an odd prime and suppose prime \(q\) divides

\[
M_p=2^p-1.
\]

Then

\[
2^p\equiv1\pmod q.
\]

The multiplicative order \(r=\operatorname{ord}_q(2)\) divides \(p\).
For a nontrivial Mersenne factor, \(r\ne1\); since \(p\) is prime,
\(r=p\). Fermat's theorem also gives \(r\mid q-1\), hence

\[
p\mid q-1.
\]

Because \(q\) is odd, (q-1) is even, so for some positive integer \(k\),

\[
q-1=2kp,
\qquad
q=2kp+1.
\]

Conversely, if the candidate \(q=2kp+1\) is prime and the exact order
check returns

\[
\operatorname{ord}_q(2)=p,
\]

then \(2^p\equiv1\pmod q\) and therefore \(q\mid M_p\). The
implementation may certify the modular closure with
\texttt{pow(2,p,q)=1} together with the declared prime/order conditions.
It never needs to materialize the enormous integer \(2^p-1\).

\subsection{Results received, 20--21 October 2026: the FEOP local operator and the bound}
\label{v3c22:received}
The FEOP transfer operator (classes S, X, Z, T; a $4\times4$ split-level matrix $A$ and a no-split matrix $B$ normalised by $q=p^{-2}$; schedule $h=j\cdot e$ with $e=v_p(Q)$ per offset) was re-derived four times and verified by two code bases with predictions first at $p=11,13,17,19,23$, at $p=5$ with $L=4$, and on joint moduli with 11 and 13 (ironclad H002759, H002761).\cite{v3feop} Its spectrum is $(B-I)(B-qI)=0$ and $(A-I)(A-qI)^2=0$ with a Jordan block; the remainder is $N\cdot R=g/(p+1)$ from $L\ge g-1$; the joint recurrence has roots $\prod_{p\in S}p^{-2}$; the stationary $(g+2)$ form and the four-state form are reconciled by an explicit map $\Phi$. The operator fails only where two offsets share a class, under whole-cluster death, when $p\nmid Q$, or under a wrong per-offset schedule; the children at a split are Z 1, T 1, dead $2(p-1)$, settled $p^2-2p$. For the bound BOUND\_C02, which remains a candidate, the runs gave a depth-uniform two-sided bracket with native receipts for its two steps on small cases, an exact Selberg remainder in its frame (polynomials $4T^3+19T^2+12T+1$ at $z=7$ and $14T^3+34T^2+15T+1$ at $z=11$), the finding that the measured joint discrepancy is neither additive across primes nor linear in $T$, and a fibering helper that carries native coverage past the 64-row source-word limit with partition receipts. The operator is the stated route to the bound's deeper coverage; no run has addressed the bound since 20 October 2026.
