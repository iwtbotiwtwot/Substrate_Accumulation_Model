\bookchapter{Starbreaker: Formation on Its Own
Grammar}\label{viii-ch08-starbreaker-formation-on-its-own-grammar}

Starbreaker is the first major formation application in this volume. It
receives its own ledgers, events, stages and occurrence views. The
useful transfer from exact computation is a routing discipline, not
substitution of the N100 graph. Both successful reconstructions and
failed physical or numerical controls belong in the account.

The account below follows the chapter source as retained at the fixed 8
September 2026 SAMA revision. Engine names, installed interfaces and
campaign status are historical source statements. They do not describe a
freshly inspected October deployment. Later source amendments are
distinguished where available; no later engine or campaign is inferred.

\subsection{Overview}\label{viii-ch08-overview}

Starbreaker is a formation application with its own native grammar. It
retains 2,976 ledgers, 482,112 typed atoms, seed/collapse/final stages,
two event orders, two D18 pair maps, same and reversed occurrence views,
contact incidence, record origin and constrained M126/N144/N144+d/L162
readouts. N100 contributes structure-first routing discipline; its
100-spin graph is not substituted for this application.

The exact state ladder begins with M126 retention. Fee-exempt Theta18
provides an independent 18-coordinate lift, so
\(2^{126}\cdot2^{18}=2^{144}\). L162 is an ambient occurrence view whose
valid image still contains \(2^{144}\) states, not \(2^{162}\)
independent matter configurations. Active mismatch coordinates travel in
an N144+d sidecar rather than being promoted to another object or
capacity.

The structure-first replay detects those native quotient, fiber and
readout certificates. It reproduces the completed parent byte for byte,
passes 78/78 checks and never invokes dense H14F fallback. The W9
history refinement adds a rank-161 target-local order-history receipt,
but the mode is emit-only: it may record cross-stage memory and may not
alter scheduler grouping or allocation without a separate tested
promotion.

The Courtroom evidence supplies a complete methodology chain. CR210
reconstructs a scoped external-collapse directional-contact surface.
CR211 freezes a typed 162-slot particle-ledger interface while leaving
address-to-template incidence null. CR005c identifies why stage ordering
cannot be promoted to physical escape time. CR005d builds a

stops on a numerical trace-residue gate; CR005f freezes one exact
trace-free correction; CR005g runs the unchanged morphology design and
