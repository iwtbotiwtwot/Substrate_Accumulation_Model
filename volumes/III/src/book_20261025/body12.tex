\bookchapter{Relational Composition and the Boundary of the
Volume}\label{viii-ch12-relational-composition-and-the-boundary-of-the-volume}

The closing chapter of the retained foundation changes evidential level. It preserves two
source-originated proposals about composition and interaction, rather
than presenting a newly validated universal law. Their role is to state
the intended conceptual relation among the finite objects already
developed. The volume can end with these questions explicit; they do not
create an obligation to run another campaign.

The account below follows the chapter source as retained at the fixed 8
September 2026 SAMA revision. Engine names, installed interfaces and
campaign status are historical source statements. They do not describe a
freshly inspected October deployment. Later source amendments are
distinguished where available; no later engine or campaign is inferred.

\subsection{Orientation}\label{viii-ch12-orientation}

SAM already contains three ingredients that are usually discussed at
different scales: a nonzero accumulation field sourced by matter, an
exact W8/X1/W9 write lifecycle with Theta carrier structure, and the
accumulated effect of many stellar sources in galaxy halos. The Nonzero
Amplification Principle identifies these as one recursive mechanism.

The central statement is:

\begin{quote}
\textbf{Matter writes. The substrate carries. Nonzero writes
accumulate.}
\end{quote}

Matter does not have to occupy the route between two sources for
information to cross that route. A source changes the substrate
accumulation; the lifted substrate carries the source-conditioned write
through matter-free space. The same composition that turns
particle-scale writes into a body-scale presence can turn stellar writes
into a galactic accumulation halo.

This document records an owner-originated conceptual bridge for
continued mathematical development. It does not assign a test result
classification.

\subsection{The information-bearing
substrate}\label{viii-ch12-the-information-bearing-substrate}

Introduce provisional notation for the source-conditioned lift:

\[
\Delta A_S(x)=A_{\mathrm{total},S}(x)-A_0.
\]

This notation distinguishes three states that must not be collapsed:

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Matter present.} Meaning: A local material source participates
  in creating or changing the write..
\item
  \textbf{Matter absent and \texttt{Delta\ A\_S(x)\textgreater{}0}.}
  Meaning: The substrate remains source-conditioned and carries
  information without an intervening material messenger..
\item
  \textbf{\texttt{Delta\ A\_S(x)=0}, equivalently the source route has
  returned to \texttt{A=A\_0}.} Meaning: The active source-conditioned
  connection is absent at that point..
\end{itemize}

The substrate is therefore not a passive geometric backdrop. Changes in
\texttt{A} change the local write environment. In the owner-originated
mechanism, those changes continually affect the W8/X1/W9 lifecycle and
Theta carrier activity throughout the connected matter-free region.

Define the source-conditioned contact domain provisionally by

\[
\Omega_S=\{x:\Delta A_S(x)>0\}.
\]

A route between two systems is in active source contact when it remains
in the appropriate connected component of this lifted domain. A point at
which the source-conditioned lift returns to zero supplies the proposed
disconnection condition.

\subsection{Exact composition rather than point-particle
messaging}\label{viii-ch12-exact-composition-rather-than-point-particle-messaging}

For a composite source \texttt{S}, write

\[
\Delta A_S(x)
=
\mathcal C_S\!\left(\{\delta A_i(x):i\in S\}\right),
\]

where each constituent contribution is nonzero on its active route and
\texttt{mathcal\ C\_S} denotes the exact composition law.

The symbol \texttt{mathcal\ C\_S} is deliberately not replaced by
ordinary linear addition. SAM has an Exact Write, retained histories,
typed carriers and directional computation. The correct composition law
should be recovered or derived from that algebra so that cross terms,
history and carrier structure remain intact.

The conceptual content is nevertheless exact enough to distinguish the
mechanisms:

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  an individual Earth particle does not send an isolated command to an
  individual Moon particle;
\item
  all particle writes in Earth compose into an Earth-scale accumulated
  write;
\item
  all particle writes in the Moon compose into a Moon-scale accumulated
  write; and
\item
  their source-conditioned domains overlap in the matter-free substrate,
  where continuing changes in \texttt{A}, W8/X1/W9 and Theta provide
  local contact.
\end{itemize}

The Moon responds inside the Earth's accumulated substrate write while
also contributing its own write to the same substrate.

\subsection{One recursive bridge across three
scales}\label{viii-ch12-one-recursive-bridge-across-three-scales}

\subsubsection{Quantum to macroscopic}\label{viii-ch12-quantum-to-macroscopic}

The quantum bridge is the passage from exact local substrate
transactions to a coherent composite source:

\[
\{\text{particle Exact Writes}\}
\xrightarrow{\mathcal C}
\text{body-scale accumulated write}.
\]

Object-level informational presence need not be introduced as an
unrelated primitive if it is the coherent closure of the constituent
writes.

\subsubsection{Earth to Moon}\label{viii-ch12-earth-to-moon}

Earth and Moon participate in one shared lifted substrate. Their
continuing internal particle activity changes their composite writes;
those changes are carried through matter-free space by the substrate
itself. The orbital response is therefore downstream of an
information-bearing write process, not merely a description of the
resulting path.

\subsubsection{Stars to galaxy}\label{viii-ch12-stars-to-galaxy}

The galactic bridge applies the same composition recursively:

\[
\{\text{stellar body writes}\}
\xrightarrow{\mathcal C}
\text{galaxy-scale accumulated halo}.
\]

Each star contributes a nonzero effect at large distance. Billions of
such contributions produce the extended galactic accumulation structure.
The particle-to-body and star-to-galaxy steps are thus two applications
of the same nonzero-preserving composition law.

\subsection{Relational interaction and completed
composition}\label{viii-ch12-relational-interaction-and-completed-composition}

\subsection{Orientation}\label{viii-ch12-orientation-1}

The first Nonzero Amplification document proposes that constituent
substrate writes can compose recursively from particles to bodies and
from stellar bodies to galaxies. The relational proposal adds the
following intended correction: composition depends on how the
constituents interact.

Two planets light-years apart and the same two planets closely orbiting
have the same constituent inventory. They do not have the same
reciprocal history, overlap, directional relation or completed substrate
write. The nearby orbiting pair forms a coherent composite source; the
distant pair approaches two separately carried source writes.

The central statement is:

\begin{quote}
\textbf{Nonzero amplification is relational. Interacting writes create
completed information that is absent from an inventory-only sum.}
\end{quote}

\subsection{Inventory and interaction are differently
typed}\label{viii-ch12-inventory-and-interaction-are-differently-typed}

Let \texttt{S} be a composite system. Its source data includes at least:

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  constituent writes \texttt{\{w\_i\}};
\item
  a typed active relation network \texttt{R\_S};
\item
  retained reciprocal or directional history \texttt{H\_S};
\item
  the local and route-dependent accumulation state; and
\item
  the completed W8/X1/W9 and Theta carrier state.
\end{itemize}

The proposed completed composition is therefore written schematically as

\[
\Delta A_S
=
\mathcal C_S\!\left(\{w_i\},R_S,H_S\right).
\]

An inventory-only control would instead evaluate

\[
\Delta A_{\mathrm{diag}}=\sum_i\delta A_i.
\]

The difference

\[
\mathcal I_S
=
\Delta A_S-\Delta A_{\mathrm{diag}}
\]

is a useful diagnostic representation of the interaction innovation. It
does not define \texttt{mathcal\ C\_S} as ordinary addition. The native
composition must retain Exact Write types, histories, cross terms and
completion.

\subsection{Separation changes the completed
write}\label{viii-ch12-separation-changes-the-completed-write}

For the same two constituents, consider two relational states.

\subsubsection{Distant state}\label{viii-ch12-distant-state}

The two source-conditioned writes are carried separately. Their active
overlap and continuing reciprocal history are reduced, and a route
returning to the universal floor supplies the proposed disconnection
condition.

\subsubsection{Nearby orbiting state}\label{viii-ch12-nearby-orbiting-state}

Each source continually changes the accumulation environment of the
other. Those changes alter the matter-free substrate between them, the
local W8/X1/W9 lifecycle and Theta carrier/intersection activity. The
pair therefore generates a continuing relational history and becomes a
new composite source.

The distinction is not ``two sources versus more sources.'' It is:

\[
(w_1,w_2,R_{\mathrm{distant}},H_{\mathrm{distant}})
\neq
(w_1,w_2,R_{\mathrm{orbit}},H_{\mathrm{orbit}}).
\]

\subsection{Many-body amplification}\label{viii-ch12-many-body-amplification}

For many particles, the number of possible relations grows faster than
the constituent count. Pair, cycle, face and higher closure information
can therefore become load-bearing even when every individual particle
write is small.

A schematic cluster display is

\[
\Delta A_S
\sim
\sum_i A_i
+\sum_{i<j}\mathcal I_{ij}
+\sum_{i<j<k}\mathcal I_{ijk}
+\cdots,
\]

but the Exact Write composition owns the actual algebra. The display
only shows what a spreadsheet-style constituent sum omits: interaction
terms whose values depend on the completed relational state.

This gives Nonzero Amplification two inseparable aspects:

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  each constituent contributes a nonzero write; and
\item
  coherent interaction among those writes generates additional completed
  information.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{ATOM3D consequence}\label{viii-ch12-atom3d-consequence}

ATOM3D has frequently represented candidate assemblies through counts,
independent relation rows and separately scored receipt features. The
current Li-6 action-frame frontier exposes the limit of that
representation: complete W9P custody retains each relation but supplies
no relation-to-frame identity, leaving 13,824 reciprocal frame
assignments.

Relational Nonzero Amplification changes the unit evaluated by a
successor. The unit should be the whole active relation network, with
each relation read in the context of all co-active writes:

\[
(e,f_e,R_S,H_S,\Delta A_S,\Theta_S)
\longmapsto
\mathcal I_e(S).
\]

Here \texttt{e} is a relation, \texttt{f\_e} its action frame and
\texttt{mathcal\ I\_e(S)} its context-dependent innovation inside the
completed assembly. The same isolated edge placed in a different network
need not produce the same innovation.

The direct successor is therefore an \textbf{interaction-aware
composite-write receipt}, not another isolated edge selector. Candidate
Li-6 assemblies can then be compared by exact completed closure while
preserving the current bars against geometry fitting and opaque
selection.

\subsection{RH consequence}\label{viii-ch12-rh-consequence}

The corresponding RH distinction is between eventwise inventory and
completed coupling. If \texttt{W} is assembled from event writes
\texttt{W\_i}, then

\[
W^*W
=
\sum_i W_i^*W_i
+\sum_{i\ne j}W_i^*W_j.
\]

The off-diagonal products carry relational information. Current RH work
already retains completed Gamma--prime--Robin coupling where eventwise
positivity is insufficient, and its support extensions are expressed
through transported carriers and entrant Schur innovations.

Relational Nonzero Amplification supplies a shared candidate origin for
those cross terms: they are the mathematical receipt of coexisting
source writes interacting inside one completed substrate history.
