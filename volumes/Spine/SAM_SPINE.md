## How to read this document

Four levels of statement are kept apart, as Volume III's reader's contract keeps them. An **identity** follows from displayed definitions and declared inputs
and is reproducible natively as an exact word or a certified matrix operation. A **recorded result** is a receipt or a comparison quoted from its record with
the record named; nothing in this document is rerun. A **reading** is the lead's summary of what a run or a chapter showed, labelled as a reading. A **transfer**
is a ledger line: an idea, method or instrument that moved from one work to another, with its date, what it produced on arrival and what it did not. Physical
interpretation is not attempted here; it belongs to the volumes and to the owner's decisions.

A thread is admitted to this document only if the same object has been built natively, or recorded as a result, in at least two of the three volumes. Six
threads pass that test. Each is written in the same five parts: where it appears, by volume and chapter; what moved, dated; what it produced; what it did
not produce; the open join, with the register entry or chapter that owns it.

Two rules from the owner govern the whole. The volumes are **material to learn from, not statements under test** (Sean Brady, 20–21 October 2026); no run here
is an examination of a volume and no number below is a grade. And **the works are separate**: Volume I's accumulation, Volume II's matter, Volume III's
computation and the Reciprocal History program each keep their own premises; the connection between them is traffic, recorded as traffic (22 October 2026).
