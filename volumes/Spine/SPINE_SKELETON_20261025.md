works; the spine is the record of traffic between them. Engineering labels only; the manuscripts' own classification phrases are
quoted as theirs. Directed by Sean Brady. Executed with Claude Code. Status: skeleton awaiting Sean Brady's approval; no manuscript
text moves until the Volume I trim plan is approved separately (CLAUDE.md Tier 3; 22 October rule).

Sources the spine is written from, and nothing else: the living texts (`volumes/I`, `volumes/II`, `volumes/III`, `volumes/Engine`),

## 3. What the spine will not do

- It will not claim unity. Each thread is a ledger of traffic; the volumes remain separate works and keep their own premises.
- It will not move manuscript text. The Volume I trim is a separate written plan awaiting approval; until then the spine references
  chapters as they stand.
- It will not issue classifications or rerun results; numbers are quoted from their records with the record named.
- It will not open external data. Measured bindings, sirens, DESI and Planck comparisons stay closed until Sean Brady's word.
