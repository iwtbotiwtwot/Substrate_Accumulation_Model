\begin{minipage}{0.97\textwidth}\noindent\textbf{Abstract.} This chapter expands the thermal-history and microwave-background test of the selected SAM inventory. It separates internally implemented hydrogen transport from the standard-physics full-spectrum lane, derives the optical-depth and acoustic bookkeeping, preserves the reported numerical receipts, and corrects their evidential interpretation. The remaining obligations are a converged recombination calculation, a unique drag ruler, a governing perturbation law, and fully specified likelihood comparisons. The native receipt remains a percent-level calculation with documented atomic and time-variable corrections. The full-shape receipts are compatibility checks of a selected packet under inherited transfer physics, rather than independent derivations of that physics. The published second-peak comparator is corrected, and the ACT temperature residual is retained explicitly. New analytical diagnostics distinguish moving recombination endpoints from background-integral changes and show why a visibility peak alone cannot certify the entire ionization history. No historical simulation or likelihood is rerun in this expansion; the numerical execution evidence still required is listed alongside clear validation and failure conditions.\end{minipage}
