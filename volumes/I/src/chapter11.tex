\begin{minipage}{0.97\textwidth}\noindent\textbf{Abstract.} The carrier partition supplies three compact proposals: a radiated-energy ceiling of one eighth of an initial source budget, a fundamental frequency coefficient $3/8$, and a damping coefficient $4/45$. Their arithmetic is exact, but their interpretation as gravitational radiation requires a source-energy map, a physical unit chart and a causal field boundary condition. This chapter proves the ceiling under explicit reservoir premises, derives an illustrative outgoing-flux identity, and formulates the quasinormal-mode problem with a frequency-dependent surface response. It distinguishes the rational matches from the Schwarzschild spectrum, includes the weak-field binary-radiation obligation, and explains why spin, excitation and mass inference matter for observation. A worked finite pulse and independent arithmetic checks clarify which results are newly established analytically and which remain source-record reports. The same boundary law must ultimately account for the thermal behavior of Chapter VII and the perturbative response studied here.\end{minipage}
