\begin{minipage}{0.97\textwidth}\noindent\textbf{Abstract.} A Home is a closed two-dimensional ledger with an interior side and a parent-domain exterior side. What an exterior observer calls bulk volume is read as ordered information on that surface, and the surface's own completion can become the floor of a new interior, so that the construction continues as an endless chain of closed spheres, each the exterior of the next. This chapter carries the idea as an idea: the custody recurrence $E_{n+1}=E_n+I_n-O_n$ with its nonnegative exterior account, the conditional radius floor, the distinction between gross output and net growth, surface response, quantum custody and what an exterior observer can learn, the re-indexing of a completed boundary into a new floor, the formation-transfer model and its counterexample to an unsupported inference, the entropy ledger for receipts, and a finite program for deciding the fate proposal. The text is that of Sections 9--19 of the submitted Chapter XII, unchanged, renumbered as a chapter of its own.\end{minipage}
