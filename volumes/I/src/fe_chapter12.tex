\begin{minipage}{0.97\textwidth}\noindent\textbf{Abstract.} The closing chapter brings together two different structures: a line-of-sight accumulation law with saturation $1/\pi$, and a matter-inventory assignment with the same numerical value. Their equality is exact within the declared count rules, but a cosmological future requires a time-evolution law and a calibrated observable. We distinguish a distance-fit coefficient from the fractional expansion rate, derive the conditions under which the original $\Omega_\Lambda^{3/2}$ expression is meaningful, and formulate finite-redshift tests. The completed-boundary or Home continuation is then developed as an explicit conditional ledger, with conservation, radius-floor and information-channel requirements. A worked receipt sequence demonstrates growth while emitting, and a differential distance test exposes the assumptions hidden in a simple rate rescaling. The resulting closure program connects the thermal boundary, radiation response and cosmological inventory without treating their common numerical factors as a substitute for dynamics. No transition mechanism or future cosmological solution is claimed to have been established by count identities alone.\end{minipage}
