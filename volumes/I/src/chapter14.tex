\begin{minipage}{0.97\textwidth}\noindent\textbf{Abstract.} This supplement retains, unchanged in text and in their original section numbering, the parts of the 19 October 2026 edition of Volume I that the 25 October 2026 edition retires from the body of the volume on Sean Brady's decision: the whole of Chapter X on many-source accumulation and galaxy halos, and Sections 6 and 9--19 of Chapter XII on the fate coefficient, the completed Home and the crossing postulate. They are retired because the targets they pursue (the halo coordinate $10/\pi$, the fate coefficient $H_0\Omega_\Lambda^{3/2}$, the completed-Home re-indexing and recurrence, and the crossing postulate) await their own foundations and are not premises of the program; the halo work remains of interest and is less connected to the rest of the volume than the other extensions. Nothing here is withdrawn. A retired target returns to the body of the volume when a construction supplies its foundation. The SAM Research Project archive holds the SPARC campaign receipts cited in Chapter X and the historical Home rules H000791 and H000794--H000797.\end{minipage}
