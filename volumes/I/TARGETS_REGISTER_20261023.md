## How the register is used

Each entry is a destination, not a verdict. When a construction appears, the entry records it and the check runs in the verification form (predictions first,
separate code, failures kept). When a check passes, the status word is Sean Brady's to change. Entries T-10 and T-11 already carry a result from this register's own
day; the others wait on the roadmap items that name them (ranking items 0–3; the boundary chapter; the source-measure construction).
