\subsection{Reference-invariant isotope contrasts and an exact anchor repair}
\label{sec:reference-stencils-12}

The empirical targets in this chapter retain their historical conventions.
On matched atomic masses the CR245 proxy $(Au-m_{\rm atom})c^2$ and the
neutral-atom convention $(Zm_H+Nm_n-m_{\rm atom})c^2$ differ by an affine
function of $Z,N$. This permits a reference-invariant comparison without
declaring the two raw targets identical. Let $X_i=(1,Z_i,N_i)$. A signed
row $w$ is invariant under every such affine shift if and only if
\begin{equation}
 wX=0,\qquad
 w(e+\alpha\mathbf1+\beta Z+\gamma N)=we.
\end{equation}
The converse follows by varying the three independent shifts.

On the actual 253-row roster there are 176 admitted local contrasts:
61 fixed-$Z$ neutron second differences at step one, 68 at step two and
47 $Z/N$ plaquettes at step two. The first use weights $(1,-2,1)$;
the plaquettes use $(1,-1,-1,1)$. All 528 frozen reference/spin/tensor
contractions reproduce, with no synthetic isotope added. There is no
unit-step $Z/N$ plaquette, and the thirteen light/anchor endpoints contain
no fixed-$Z$ three-point stencil. The complete support lists and values
are retained with the report and its exact source arrays.

For the asymmetry shape $f_Z(N)=(N-Z)^2/(N+Z)$, write
$f=A-4Z+4Z^2/A$. The affine terms cancel, so
\begin{equation}
 f_Z(N+h)-2f_Z(N)+f_Z(N-h)
 =\frac{8Z^2h^2}{A(A^2-h^2)},\qquad A>h.
\end{equation}
This is curvature of the source shape, not a complete binding law.
Retained mass uncertainties and folds are correlated inputs; a sum of
absolute weighted uncertainties is not a confidence interval. These
full-fit contrasts are descriptive, not a newly held-chain score. For
variational endpoint intervals a signed row uses lower endpoints on
positive weights and upper endpoints on negative weights for its lower,
reversing for its upper. Differences of trial uppers alone do not bound
a physical separation energy.

The three original free-constituent anchors also correct a literal
overstatement in the preceding discussion. Their stored $(A,\text{surface},C)$
matrix has exact rank three. The weights
\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
 a&=278070783625/51574868504,\qquad b_s=-a,\\
 c&=\frac{35563788859572595628875}{17880008275196452753326}
\end{split}
\end{equation}
interpolate H1,H2,He3 with zero rational residual; approximately they are
$(5.3915946214,-5.3915946214,1.9890253020)$.
Thus impossibility at \emph{any} weighting is false. The tested global
and out-of-fold fits still show the stated accuracy trade-off against
these anchors. Exact interpolation supplies neither a bulk/held score
nor the missing physical source/action mechanism.
