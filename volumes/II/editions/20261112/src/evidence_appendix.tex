\subsection{Common energy reference}

The original positive incidence quadratic, the complete-square He3 field
and the Li6 linear-density scalar field are separately specified laws.
The negative density term in the latter follows from its full square
completion and the absence of a compensating density counterterm. A change
of law must propagate through both the joined upper and every fragment
lower. The receiving-action family gives examples of this propagation,
including a changed dynamics with the same static kernel.

The finite actual-source margin at $g^2=33/2$ uses the $79\tau/10$ lower,
whereas the unchanged14336 result at $g^2=35$ uses the $8\tau$ lower. The
energy gains are not combined. The separate calibration, finite-time
loading, battery/clock and thermal margins have different error hypotheses;
none is a measured binding value. Electronic and atomic endpoints remain
under the common reference defined in Chapter~XVI.
