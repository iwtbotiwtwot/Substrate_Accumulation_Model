## 2. Critical moments with an error bound uniform in N

This section reconstructs the static lemma instead of treating it as a fitted
scaling law. Completing the Gaussian square gives

    exp(M²/(2N))=sqrt(N/(2π))∫exp(−Ny²/2+yM)dy.

After summing the spins, the exact auxiliary field Y has density proportional
to exp[Nφ(y)], where φ(y)=log cosh y−y²/2. Conditional on Y=y, the N spins
are independent and have mean tanh y. This is an exact representation of (1),
including the normalization factor e^−1/2 2^N sqrt(N/(2π)); it does not replace
the dynamics by an auxiliary-field process.

Choose fixed sufficiently small δ>0. Taylor's theorem and continuity give
finite constants C_6,c_0>0 such that on |y|≤δ,

    |φ(y)+y⁴/12|≤C_6|y|⁶,    φ(y)≤−c_0y⁴.        (7)

ratio and the minimum of −φ(y)/y⁴, with its continuous value 1/12 at zero.

on |y|≥4, log cosh y≤|y| implies φ≤−y²/4.

Put U=N^1/4Y. Both exponents in the inner range are at most −c u⁴, with

    |exp[Nφ(uN^−1/4)]−exp(−u⁴/12)|

a Gaussian tail starting at |u|=4N^1/4, also exponentially small compared with

by O_p(N^−1/2), with a constant independent of N. The denominator tends to
the strictly positive quartic integral, so division preserves that error.
Finitely many smaller N can be absorbed into the same constant. In particular,

    E U²=c_*+O(N^−1/2),   E|U|^(2j)=O_j(1),
    c_*=sqrt(12)Γ(3/4)/Γ(1/4).                     (9)

The gamma ratio follows directly by z=u⁴/12 in the two even integrals.

Let a=tanh Y and Z=m−a. Conditional independence gives exactly

    E[m²|Y]=a²+(1−a²)/N.                          (10)

E a²=c_*/sqrt(N)+O(N^−1). For a fixed positive integer j, expand the

    E[|Z|^(2j)|Y]≤C_j N^−j,

    μ2:=E m²=c_*/sqrt(N)+O(N^−1),

## 2. Hankel geometry and Gauss exactness (C01)

For m>=1 and polynomial p(lambda)=sum_{j=0}^{m-1} p_j lambda^j, put

Suppose G>0. Compression of multiplication by lambda onto polynomials of degree
<m has matrix B=G^(-1)D. It is self-adjoint in the G inner product because
GB=D=D^T. Its eigenvalues lie in the support's closed convex hull by Rayleigh
quotients. The identities B e_j=e_(j+1), j<m-1, make e_0 cyclic. A self-adjoint
matrix with a cyclic vector has simple eigenvalues and nonzero projection of
that vector onto every eigenvector: otherwise an eigenspace contains a vector
orthogonal to every B^j e_0. Thus its m nodes xi_i are distinct and its weights
w_i=||E_i e_0||_G² are strictly positive, with sum_i w_i=mu_0=1.

## 5. Exact binary law and defect (C02)
