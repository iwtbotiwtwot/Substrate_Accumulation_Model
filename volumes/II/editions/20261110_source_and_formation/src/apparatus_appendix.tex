\subsection{Local baths and retained information}

The spectral reset is an added many-body receiving law. A candidate based
on local physical densities and bounded field Weyl operators has the desired
Gibbs stationary states and full flavor fixed algebra when its interacting
Bohr components and thermal rates are supplied. A finite energy-relaxation
rate and a microscopic realization of those components remain open.
Ordinary bare $\mathcal D[b]$ loss does not implement interacting Gibbs
cooling. Freezing matter while cooling only the field also preserves every
density probability and cannot create the required orbital rearrangement.

Likewise, swapping in an already prepared interacting Gibbs copy transfers
the resource whose formation was at issue; its preparation and reset must
be charged. In every dissipative implementation the full system-bath
isometry can retain source information that the reduced Gibbs state loses.
The bath outputs, failed arrivals, work-store correlations and source
replacement records are therefore part of the same formation account.
These conditional families specify concrete tests of rate, response,
information and energy for a subsequent physical implementation.
