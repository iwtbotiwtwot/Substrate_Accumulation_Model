\subsection{Source information must accompany source-dependent work}
\label{sec:apparatus-obstruction-new}

Suppose an energy-conserving apparatus implements an exact isometry from
$F_i$ to $F_f$ on every source input, beginning and ending in fixed pure
apparatus states independent of that input. Conservation requires
\begin{equation}
F_i^*HF_i-F_f^*HF_f=(E_{A,f}-E_{A,i})I.
\label{eq:apparatus-obstruction-new}
\end{equation}
The required source-compressed energy change is therefore scalar. At the
held point, the actual phase9 matrix has off-diagonal $(0,5)$ entry
$6379/675$. Equation~\eqref{eq:apparatus-obstruction-new} fails before
the source is enlarged to 600 directions.

A faithful implementation can retain source/work-store correlations,
accept a quantified approximation with a coherent reservoir, or state a
smaller input family. Tracing out a common environment while demanding an
exact pure reduced isometry cannot conceal the information. For two pure
apparatus outputs of overlap $c$ and energy standard deviations
$\sigma_1,\sigma_2$, the useful estimate is
\begin{equation}
|\Delta E|\,|c|\leq(\sigma_1+\sigma_2)\sqrt{1-|c|^2}.
\end{equation}
High overlap at different output energies requires energy spread in the
apparatus. That spread is an initial resource, distinct from irreversible
consumption. Appendix~\ref{app:apparatus} develops the retained battery and
clock constructions with their explicit resource hypotheses.
