\clearpage\markboth{Reading this edition}{}\section*{Reading this edition}
The revised edition connects the retained matter and spin constructions to
the completed nuclear-source and formation work of 8--10 November 2026.
Its central sequence is source, complete energy comparison, preparation
protocol and independent physical response. The 5 November manuscript and
its scientific predecessors are preserved separately.

Part I establishes finite admission, particle grammar, mass channels, the
nuclear ledger and the retained binding benchmark. Part II develops exact
spin sources, hidden memory, composition and the complete physical response
receiver. Part III carries these objects into nuclear, atomic and molecular
construction and the common-energy physical map. Binary contact coordinates,
physical particle inventories and electronic spin labels retain their own
source definitions.

Chapter XI now includes the verified relaxed-He3 complete-payment intervals,
the earlier free-reference and shared-field covariance theorems, the actual
He4/H2 receiver, and the verified source-connected Li6 energy certificates.
The latter use one explicitly declared scalar Hamiltonian for joined matter
and every proper fragment. The selected hopping construction and the
flavor-preserving unchanged preparation are distinct receiving maps.

Chapter XII develops source-compatible arrival, flavor-preserving controls,
full-Fock finite-time field loading and its signed work account. Chapter XVI
turns the proposed interaction into independent response tests: legal states
with equal incidence currents, an eleven-state static-kernel design, flavor
vertices, dynamic poles and a quantitative calibration tolerance. Appendix A
retains the longer coherent-battery, clock and thermal constructions with
their apparatus hypotheses. Appendix B records verification scope and source
custody.

The He3, free-reference, covariance and nine-claim Li6 cases retain their
internal separate-proof/code verification. The newest arrival, loading,
response and reservoir constructions retain their analytical and internal
computational evidence without acquiring a new independent case status.
A declared Hamiltonian, a variational energy certificate, an implementable
formation law and a calibrated isotope prediction remain different stages
of the same program. Their precise interfaces are developed in the chapters.

Earlier source laws and fitted or held comparisons keep their original
meaning. In particular the historical scalar-current helium test in
Chapter XVI is distinct from the new scalar cavity benchmark. The spin
section retains its source Dirichlet, dense-source and stopped/partial
calculation records. No scientific campaign, binding fit or physical unit
assignment was performed for this editorial revision. The editable edition
includes baseline hashes, selected evidence snapshots, a coverage map and
reproducible build instructions.
